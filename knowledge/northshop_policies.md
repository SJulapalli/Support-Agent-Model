# NorthShop Support Policies

## Return & Refund Policy

NorthShop uses a tiered refund system based on the reason for the return and how long ago the order was delivered.

### Refund tiers

**Tier 1 — Full refund (100%)**
- Applies when: the reason is a product issue (product defect, wrong item received, or damaged in transit) AND the order was delivered within the last 30 days.
- No item return required for orders under $200.
- For orders over $200, the customer may be asked to submit a photo of the damage.

**Tier 2 — Partial refund (75%)**
- Applies when: the reason is a product issue (product defect, wrong item received, or damaged in transit) AND the order was delivered 31–60 days ago.
- The delay in reporting reduces the refund to 75% of the order total.
- Example: order placed 45 days ago with a product defect → refund = 75% of order total.

**Tier 3 — Partial refund (50%)**
- Applies when: the reason is customer preference (changed mind or other non-product reason) AND the order was delivered within the last 30 days.
- A 50% restocking/processing fee applies for preference-based returns.
- Example: customer changed their mind within 30 days → refund = 50% of order total.

**No refund**
- Reason is customer preference (changed mind / other) AND the order is more than 30 days old.
- Any reason AND the order is more than 60 days old.
- Orders that have already been refunded (one refund per order).

**Escalate to human agent**
- Any product-issue claim on an order older than 60 days.
- Order value exceeds $500 and customer is requesting a refund.

### Eligibility requirements (all tiers)
- The order must have a status of "delivered".
- Only one refund is permitted per order.
- Digital goods and gift cards are non-refundable.

### How to process a refund
1. Confirm the customer's order number.
2. Look up the order in the portal to verify status, order date, and total.
3. Determine the appropriate refund tier based on the customer's stated reason and order age.
4. Open Actions → Issue Refund. Select the category that matches the customer's reason, provide details, and enter the correct partial amount if Tier 2 or Tier 3 applies.
5. For Tier 1, leave the amount field blank (defaults to full refund).
6. For Tier 2, enter 75% of the order total as the partial amount.
7. For Tier 3, enter 50% of the order total as the partial amount.

## Shipping & Delivery

**Standard shipping (free on orders over $75):**
- Continental US: 5–7 business days
- Alaska/Hawaii: 7–10 business days

**Expedited shipping ($12.99):**
- Continental US: 2–3 business days

**International shipping:**
- Available to 30+ countries. Estimated delivery 10–21 business days depending on destination and customs processing.

**Order tracking:**
Once an order ships, customers receive a tracking number via email. Orders can also be tracked through the support agent by providing the order number.

---

## Order Status Definitions

- **Pending**: Order placed, payment processing
- **Processing**: Payment confirmed, order being prepared for shipment
- **Shipped**: Order handed to carrier, tracking number available
- **Delivered**: Order marked as delivered by carrier
- **Refunded**: Refund has been issued (may be full or partial)
- **Cancelled**: Order cancelled before shipment

---

## Damaged or Incorrect Items

If a customer receives a damaged or incorrect item:
1. The support agent should apologize and process a refund per the tier policy above.
2. No item return required for orders under $200.
3. For orders over $200, the customer may be asked to submit a photo.
4. Replacements are not offered through the portal — direct the customer to re-order if they want a replacement.

---

## Account & Order Changes

**Cancellations:**
Orders can be cancelled only if status is "pending" or "processing". Once shipped, cancellation is not possible — customer should wait for delivery and then request a refund.

**Address changes:**
Address changes are only possible if the order status is "pending" or "processing". The support agent can note the request; actual address changes require human intervention.

**Email changes:**
Customers can update their email address at any time through the support agent.

---

## Escalation

The support agent should escalate to a human agent when:
- The customer explicitly asks to speak to a human.
- The issue involves suspected fraud or account compromise.
- The order value exceeds $500 and the customer is requesting a refund.
- A product-issue refund claim is made on an order older than 60 days.
- The customer has contacted support more than 3 times about the same issue.
- The agent cannot resolve the issue within 3 browser task attempts.
